How ApeMe is built

Three places run code: the phone, Cloudflare, and one VPS. Everything else is a public API we read. Every box below has one job, and the arrows are the only ways they talk.

The app never touches a chain or a data provider directly. It talks to one API. The indexer never talks to the app. It feeds the API. That rule is what keeps the whole thing debuggable at 3am.

Two kinds of things, one example

Everything on this page is explained with the same story. Read it once and every box below makes sense.

kind 1 · the money

Stock tokens

whoxStocks, Backpack, PreStocks. They take a real share and mint a token for it on Solana.
exampleNVDAx is Nvidia, issued by xStocks. SPACEX is SpaceX, issued by PreStocks.
weRead the list once a day. Never mint, never redeem, never hold a share.
kind 2 · the casino

Launchpads

whoStonkFun on Raydium LaunchLab, pump.fun Custom Pairs, Clawpump on Meteora DBC. Memes get created here, priced in a stock token.
exampleCATNVDA is a cat meme launched on StonkFun, priced in NVDAx. Buying it costs NVDAx, not SOL.
weIndex them live. Never launch anything.

Who is responsible for what

Each piece owns one thing. If a bug is about that thing, it lives in that box, nowhere else.

the phone · swift

ApeMe iOS app

ownsEverything the user sees. Screens, the chart, the tape, the ape button, pushes.
doesReads the API. Keeps one WebSocket open to the room of the token on screen. Asks Privy to sign. Sends signed bytes back.
neverCalls a chain, a DEX, or a data provider. Holds a fee. Computes heat or PnL.
exampleJoey opens Stocks, taps Nvidia, sees CATNVDA at $41K, taps it. Chart, tape, Ape button. He taps Ape, $20 preset, done. His buy scrolls onto his own tape a second later.
talks toAPI, Live feed, Privy, APNs.
the phone · privy sdk

Privy wallet

ownsLogin and the user's Solana key.
doesEmail or Phantom login. Creates the embedded wallet. Signs the swap transaction on the device with no popup.
neverSends the key anywhere. Signs server-side, until auto-ape and only through a session signer the user granted.
exampleJoey signed up with email on day one, the wallet was made then. At 14:32 it signs the CATNVDA swap in the background. No popup, no seed phrase.
talks toThe app only.
cloudflare · hono

API

ownsThe contract with the app. Every endpoint, every response shape.
doesServes stocks, memes per stock, token page, quote, ape, me, kols, calls. Reads KV first, D1 second. Builds the swap through Jupiter with the 1% fee. Broadcasts signed transactions through Nexus RPC. Writes the fee ledger.
neverPolls anything. Holds a stream. Holds a key. Does slow work inside a request.
exampleGET /stocks returns Nvidia. GET /stocks/NVDAx/memes returns CATNVDA. POST /quote asks Jupiter for $20 NVDAx into CATNVDA with the 1% fee. POST /ape takes the signed bytes, broadcasts, writes the fee row: $0.20 fee, $0.06 to @ravi.
talks toKV, D1, Live feed, Jupiter, Nexus RPC.
cloudflare · scheduled

Crons

ownsThe catalog and the slow-moving numbers.
doesEvery minute: stock list from StonkFun, launches from LaunchLab and pump.fun filtered to stock quotes, into D1. Every 30s: DexScreener snapshots and heat into KV. Every 2 min: X reader for tracked KOLs, classifies calls, drops them on the push queue.
neverServes a request. Touches trades, that is the indexer's.
example09:00: stock list pulled, Nvidia in it. 14:17: LaunchLab list shows CATNVDA created a minute ago, token row written. Every 30s: DexScreener says CATNVDA $41K. 14:30:10: X reader sees @ravi’s post, classifier matches $CATNVDA, call goes on the queue.
talks toStonkFun, LaunchLab, pump.fun, DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, X API, D1, KV, Queue.
cloudflare · endpoint

Ingest

ownsThe only write path for trades and launch events.
doesAccepts signed batches from the indexer. Drops anything already seen by signature. Writes trades and candles to D1, bumps price and volume in KV, wakes the token's room.
neverAccepts unsigned posts. Accepts a trade from anyone but the indexer.
example14:32:05.5: a batch arrives from the indexer with Joey’s buy and three others. Signature not seen before, so: trades row, folded into the 14:32 candle, KV price bumped, CATNVDA room woken.
talks toD1, KV, Live feed.
cloudflare · durable object

Live feed

ownsReal time to the phone.
doesOne room per token. Holds every open WebSocket for it. When Ingest wakes it, fans the trade to every phone in under 100ms. Keeps the last 50 trades in memory for a fresh join.
neverStores history. Reads a provider. Does math beyond the tape.
example40 phones have CATNVDA open. The room pushes "buy 0.19 NVDAx Â· Jo…4f" to all 40 in one go. Joey’s phone is one of them.
talks toThe app, Ingest.
cloudflare · storage

KV and D1

KVHot numbers with a 30-second life: price, market cap, volume, heat, the floor order. Cheap to read a million times.
D1The record. Stocks, tokens, trades, 1-minute candles, KOLs, calls, users, positions, the fee ledger. Thousands of tokens, millions of trades, fine.
exampleKV: price and mcap for CATNVDA, refreshed every 30s. D1: the CATNVDA token row, Joey’s trade row, @ravi’s call row, the $0.06 fee row waiting for @ravi to claim.
neverKV holds anything we cannot lose. D1 sits in a hot request path when KV can answer.
cloudflare · queue

Push

ownsGetting a call onto the lock screen.
doesTakes a call from the queue, looks up who watches that KOL, sends the APNs push with an Ape button, retries on failure, records delivery.
neverDecides what a call is. Sends to users who did not opt in.
example14:30:10 the call hits the queue. The consumer finds 312 users watching @ravi and sends 312 pushes with an Ape button. Joey’s phone buzzes at 14:30:14.
talks toD1, APNs.
vps · node · systemd · six programs

Indexer microservice

ownsSeeing every trade and every launch the second it lands.
doesHolds the Yellowstone stream from Nexus. Filters to six programs: pump.fun, PumpSwap, Raydium LaunchLab and CPMM, Meteora DBC and DAMM v2. Decodes each transaction with Shyft's parser into create, buy, sell, graduate. Batches every 500ms and posts to Ingest with a signature. On restart, resumes from the last slot using 24h replay.
neverTalks to the app. Holds a database. Decides what is a stonk meme, Ingest checks the quote mint against D1.
example14:32:05 the stream delivers Joey’s transaction on the LaunchLab program. Parser decodes buy_exact_in into pool, wallet, side buy, 0.19 NVDAx in, 1.2M CATNVDA out. Batched with three other trades, posted to Ingest half a second later.
talks toNexus gRPC, Ingest. A health endpoint for us.
outside · paid

constant-k Nexus

ownsOur view of the chain, in and out.
doesStreams the six programs to the indexer. Broadcasts signed apes with priority through SWQoS. 500 requests a second, 200 sends, 20 priority sends.
neverIs the only path. Helius free is the spare RPC, polling is the spare feed.
example14:32:03 broadcasts Joey’s signed swap with priority. 14:32:05 streams the confirmed transaction back to the indexer, along with every other trade on the six programs.
talks toIndexer, API.

The four things that happen

Numbered because order is the point. Each step names the box that does it.

A new meme launches against a stock

under 1 second live, 1 minute by cron as backup
1nexusCreate instruction hits the streamon pump.fun, LaunchLab or Meteora DBC
2indexerDecoded to a create eventmint, pool, quote mint, creator
3ingestQuote mint checked against stocksnot a stock, dropped
4d1 · kvToken row written, floor order bumpedstatus: new, launchpad badge
5appCard appears under its stocknext refresh or room wake

Someone trades a meme

stream to screen in about 300ms
1nexusBuy or sell in the streamany of the four programs
2indexerDecoded, batched 500mswallet, side, amounts, price, slot
3ingestDedupe, write trade, fold into the 1m candlebump price and volume in KV
4live feedRoom fans outevery phone on that token
5appTape scrolls, candle movescallout avatars stay pinned

Someone apes

one tap, about 2 seconds to confirmed
1appTap Ape, amount presetasks API for a quote
2apiJupiter quote and swap tx1% fee set, referral account ours
3privySigned on the deviceno popup, no key leaves
4apiBroadcast through Nexus RPCpriority send, ledger row: 0.3% to the tapped KOL
5indexerThe trade comes back in the streamposition badge updates like any other trade

A KOL calls a token

post to lock screen in about 2 minutes
1cronX reader pulls new posts50 tracked handles, since last id
2cronClassifier finds a tokencashtag, mint, ticker, launch link
3d1 · queueCall row stamped with time and pricedropped on the push queue
4pushAPNs to everyone watching that KOLwith an Ape button
5appCallout pinned on the candletap opens the buy screen, that tap is the attribution

When something dies

Every single point of failure has a degraded mode. Nothing takes the app down with it.

What diesWhat the user seesWhat happens
Nexus streamTape updates every 30s instead of liveCrons keep polling LaunchLab and GeckoTerminal. Indexer reconnects with backoff and replays from the last slot.
The VPSSame as aboveIngest gets nothing, KV still refreshed by crons. Restart resumes from the last slot, 24h replay window.
Nexus RPCApes still landAPI fails over to the free Helius endpoint for sends, without priority.
A data APINothing, for hoursCatalog is in D1. A missing poll leaves stale numbers, not empty screens.
JupiterApe button says try againThe one hard dependency for trading. Quote retried across the Lite and Pro hosts.
X APICalls arrive lateReader resumes from the last post id. No call is lost, only delayed.
CloudflareApp is downAccepted. Same blast radius as every other app on Workers.
