What indexing gives us

One real token, GTA Speed, priced in TTWO (Take-Two stock). Launched on pump.fun on 13 Sep.

1 · A trade happens on chain

Someone sold Speed at 16:44:53 UTC tonight. Kaldera hands us the transaction. We decode it into one row.

time 16:44:53 · side sell · wallet EiDKVt… · sold 6,029,324 SPEED · got 1.067 TTWO price = 1.067 ÷ 6,029,324 = 0.000000174 TTWO per SPEED · × TTWO $209.68 = $0.0000365

That row is the tape on the token page. Every trade since 21:23 IST tonight is in there. 22,000 rows so far, across all tokens.

2 · Rows become candles

All trades in the same minute fold into one candle: first price, highest, lowest, last, and volume. That's what a chart is.

minute 16:44 · open 1.800e-7 · high 1.801e-7 · low 1.740e-7 · close 1.740e-7 · vol 1.08 TTWO · 3 trades the big sell above pushed low and close down. Red candle.

3 · The chart needs the token's whole life

Speed is four days old. We only started listening tonight. Left of the orange line is copied once from pump.fun's chart. Right of it is ours, built from our own rows as they arrive.

583 minutes copied, 26 minutes ours so far. From tomorrow, the green part keeps growing forever. The grey part never changes and never needs fetching again.

4 · Why the copy matters

Without itOpen Speed at 21:30 tonight: chart shows 7 minutes. Market cap fine, 24h change unknown, looks like a token born an hour ago.
With itOpen Speed: four days of price, the pump on the 17th, the 24h change, the same chart pump.fun shows. Plus our live tape they don't have.

Green rows are the product. Amber rows are a one-time copy of the past, because the chain stream only rewinds 24 hours and these tokens are days or months old. After tonight, no chart on our app is ever fetched from pump.fun or Raydium again. Ours is the only source.

6 · What each screen reads

Stocks list stocks table: 94 rows, USD price every 30s, count of memes per stock Memes for NVDAx tokens + token_stats: name, image, launchpad, price, mcap, 24h vol, buys/sells, graduated or not Token page candles_1m for the chart · trades for the tape · token_stats for the header Live each new trade pushed to the phone; it moves the last candle itself

None of these screens call Raydium, pump.fun or DexScreener. They call our database. That's the point of indexing: we own the data, so the app is fast, and we can show things nobody else does.
