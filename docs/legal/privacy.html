Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

Stonks247 is an iPhone app for trading tokenized stocks, ETFs, crypto and yield tokens on the Solana network. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted. It covers the Stonks247 iPhone app and stonks247.fun.

What we collect

  • Your email address. You sign in with Apple or with an email code. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your address, we only ever see Apple's relay address.
  • Your Solana wallet address and the trading activity tied to it — the orders you place, the swaps you make and the tokens you hold.
  • Basic technical data when the app talks to our servers: IP address, timestamps and the requests themselves. We use it to deliver the service, fix faults and block abuse.
  • Your invite code while access is invite-only, so a code can't be reused past its limit.

What stays on your phone

Your watchlist, whether you've seen the intro, and your active wallet address are stored on your device only. They are never sent to us, and deleting the app removes them.

What we do not collect

  • No tracking and no advertising. The app contains no analytics, attribution or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not build advertising profiles.
  • No device permissions. The app never asks for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or notifications, because it does not use them.
  • No private keys. See below.
  • No identity documents, bank details or card numbers. We do not ask for your name, date of birth, government ID, bank account or card, and we have no way to receive them.

Your wallet and your keys

Your wallet is created and secured by Privy, our authentication and wallet provider. Its private key is split so that no single party — Privy included — holds a usable copy, and we never see it, store it or have any way to reconstruct it. We cannot move your funds, sign a transaction for you, or recover your wallet on your behalf. Every transaction is authorised on your device.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To create your wallet, show your balances, and place and cancel the orders you ask for.
  • To show you your own order and trade history.
  • To keep the service working and secure, and to honour invite limits.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who else handles your data

We use a small number of providers to run the service. Each one only receives what it needs:

  • Privy — sign-in and wallet infrastructure. Receives your email address and wallet address.
  • Cloudflare — hosts our API and website. Processes request data including your IP address.
  • Jupiter — the on-chain protocol that routes your swaps and holds your limit orders until they fill or you cancel them. Receives your wallet address and order details as part of the transaction.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, App Store and TestFlight, under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Market data and news providers — supply prices, company information and headlines to our servers. They receive no personal data about you.

The blockchain is public and permanent

Solana is a public ledger. Once a transaction is confirmed, your wallet address and everything it has ever done are publicly visible and permanent. Anyone can read them, and neither we nor anyone else can edit or erase them. If your wallet address is ever linked to your identity, that history can be linked to you too. This is how public blockchains work, and it is worth understanding before you trade.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as you have an account, and we keep records of trading activity for as long as we are required to. Request data and logs are kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting, then discarded.

Deleting your account and your data

In the app: You → Delete account. If your wallet still holds funds the app will not delete the account until you have moved them out, because deleting the account removes the only way to sign in to that wallet. Export your private key from Privy's recovery page for Stonks247 and use it in any Solana wallet, such as Phantom, to move your funds. You can also email support@stonks247.fun from the address you signed in with and we will do it for you.

When you delete your account we remove your email address, profile, wallet list, settings and watchlist, and we delete your login with Privy. We keep an anonymised record of your trades and orders, tied only to a wallet address, because we are required to keep financial records. We also keep a one-way hash of your sign-in email so that a later support request from the same address can be matched to those records; the hash cannot be turned back into your email.

Two limits worth stating plainly. Transactions already on the Solana blockchain cannot be deleted by anyone, including us. And your funds are unaffected by deletion: they stay in your wallet on-chain, and you can export your private key at any time from Privy's recovery page for Stonks247.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, or to object to how we use it. Email support@stonks247.fun and we will action it. You will never be treated differently for asking.

Children

Stonks247 is not for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Your sign-in session is held in the iOS keychain. No system is perfectly secure, so if a breach ever affects your data we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for anything significant we will tell you in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your data: support@stonks247.fun
