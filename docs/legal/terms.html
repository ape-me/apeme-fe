What Stonks247 is

Stonks247 is a self-custody wallet and trading interface. It shows you prices and market information, and it lets you send your own instructions to public protocols on the Solana network. We do not hold your funds, take custody of your assets, act as your broker, or execute trades on your behalf. Every transaction is signed on your device, by you, and settles on-chain between you and the protocol.

What the tokens actually are

The stock and ETF names in the app are tokens issued by third parties that track the price of an underlying security. They are not shares, and holding one does not make you a shareholder. You get no voting rights, no dividend entitlement from the company, and no direct claim on the issuer of the underlying stock. Whether a token holds its peg to the price it tracks depends entirely on the third party that issues it and the liquidity available on-chain — not on us.

Pre-IPO names work the same way and are more speculative still: they track private companies whose valuations are infrequent, negotiated and illiquid.

Orders and limit orders

Market orders route through a third-party aggregator at the best price it can find at that moment. The price you end up with can differ from the price you were quoted — that is slippage, and it is normal.

Limit orders are held on-chain by the Jupiter Trigger protocol, not by us. When you place one, your funds move into that protocol's escrow and stay there until the order fills or you cancel it. Fills are performed by independent keepers. An order reaching your price does not guarantee it fills — liquidity may not be there. Minimum sizes, price limits and expiry are set by that protocol, and it can change them without notice. We show you its rules; we do not set them.
